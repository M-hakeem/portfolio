Know Me More

I'm Dr. Kabiru Atta

A strategic leader working at the intersection of digital transformation, health systems strengthening, and sustainable financing in emerging markets. Certified in PRINCE2 (UK) and a Master Trainer in PMDPro, with a combined project portfolio of over $34 million USD across multiple states in Nigeria. My work spans governance reform, maternal & child health, tuberculosis, nutrition, gender equality, WASH, and economic empowerment — grounded in systems thinking and a belief that lasting change requires leadership, financing, technology, and governance moving together with clarity and purpose.

EXPERIENCES

A Decade of Leadership & Impact

Career Journey

November 2021 — Present

Country Representative at EngenderHealth

Develop and lead the vision and strategy of EngenderHealth’s overall country program and support new business development opportunities, including interacting with corporate and other philanthropic organizations in Nigeria.

6 Years+

Head of Developments Programme at Plan International Nigeria

The role provides leadership in the design, development, implementation and evaluation of all development programs and projects in line with Country Strategic Plan. The role provides support to all projects on the development side and works closely with the Emergency Response Manager to build and sustain a development humanitarian nexus.

November 2014 - September 2015

State Project Coordinator at USAID funded FHI360 MAPS project

Provides technical assistance and management to the overall programs in Zamfara state. . Management of the program and finance sub unit heads and officers to ensure effective implementation of program.

October 2013 - November 2014

State Technical Malaria Manager at DfID funded SuNMaP project

The SuNMaP Project in Nigeria focuses on strengthening the health systems delivery of malaria efforts towards reducing malaria within the target population of women and children under five.
